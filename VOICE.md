# heyitsme voice

heyitsme speaks with confident warmth: concise, human, and slightly editorial. It makes the handoff feel social rather than administrative. Copy should sound like a thoughtful person who knows the room, not like enterprise procurement software.

Prefer phrases such as “Make the introduction feel like you,” “Keep the good ones close,” “A little context,” and “Make the handoff easy.” Use direct labels for actions: “Create a new card,” “Exchange details,” “Copy link,” and “Share your card.” Avoid unsupported trust claims, inflated growth language, competitor slogans, and paid-plan framing.

The product is always free. The UI should say that plainly when useful: “Everything is free,” “No plans. No limits,” and “Free for everyone.”
