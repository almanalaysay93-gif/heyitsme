# heyitsme voice

heyitsme speaks with confident warmth: concise, human, and slightly editorial. It makes the handoff feel social rather than administrative. Copy should sound like a thoughtful person who knows the room, not like enterprise procurement software.

Prefer phrases such as “Make the introduction feel like you,” “Keep the good ones close,” “A little context,” and “Make the handoff easy.” Use direct labels for actions: “Create a new card,” “Exchange details,” “Copy link,” and “Share your card.” Avoid unsupported trust claims, inflated growth language, competitor slogans, and pushy upgrade language.

heyitsme is freemium. Say it plainly: “Start free,” “Your card, QR code and link never expire,” “Upgrade when networking starts creating opportunities.” Never write “100% free,” “no subscriptions,” “no paywalls,” or “every feature is free.” Upgrade prompts name the reason (“You’ve used your free lead allowance this month”) and what Pro changes, then stop. Never imply data loss on Free: downgrades keep every card and contact. Avoid promising “no limits” because practical technical limits exist (file uploads up to 3MB, 20 portfolio items, 12 contact tags). Prices are in Philippine pesos with the ₱ sign.
